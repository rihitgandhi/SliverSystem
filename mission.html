Our Mission

For seven years running, the same six mistakes have caused 96% of the web's accessibility failures.

Missing labels. Empty buttons. Text nobody can read against its background. Not exotic bugs — the basics. And every year, WebAIM's audit of the internet's top one million homepages finds them again, at greater scale, on more of the web than the year before.

The scale of the problem

96% of web accessibility failures come from six mistakes.
70M+ Americans live with a disability.
95.9% of the sites they use fail basic standards.

Over 70 million Americans live with a disability. Not a niche audience — more than one in four adults. And 95.9% of the sites they use every day fail to meet basic accessibility standards.

This isn't a technology problem. The tools to fix it have existed for decades. It's an ownership problem. Fixing accessibility site-by-site means it's always someone else's job — the next sprint, the next redesign, the next developer's responsibility. Nobody owns it, so nothing changes.

AccessAI exists because we stopped waiting for the web to fix itself.

If no website is going to consistently apply your accessibility needs, then your browser should. Second Brain puts the fix where it actually works: with the person who needs it, applied automatically, everywhere they go — not filed as a ticket that never gets prioritized.

We're not asking a million websites to change. We're making sure they don't have to, for you to get what you need.
