AI can help accessibility, but only with limits.

The strongest case for AccessAI is not hype. It is the scale and repetition of the same accessibility failures across the web.

AI can reduce friction by helping apply a user's own preferences more consistently. It cannot replace standards, testing, or human verification.

WebAIM 2026

95.9% of the top 1 million home pages had detectable WCAG 2 failures.

The audit found 56.1 accessibility errors per page on average, up 10.1% from 2025.

Recurring failures

Six error types caused 96% of detected issues for seven straight years.

Contrast, missing alt text, missing form labels, empty links, empty buttons, and missing document language keep returning.

Who it affects

More than 1 in 4 U.S. adults report having a disability.

CDC 2022 data puts that number over 70 million people, rising to 43.9% among adults 65+.

AI limits

Fluent descriptions can still be wrong.

Vision-AI can describe images, but it can also hallucinate screenshot text or invent details. Accessibility tools need visible limits.
