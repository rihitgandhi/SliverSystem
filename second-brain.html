Voice Navigation

Say it.
Keep moving.

Voice Navigation uses your browser's speech-recognition capability to turn a small set of spoken commands into browser actions.

“click Submit”
“scroll down”
“focus Search”

Also supports scroll up, top, or bottom; go back or forward; and stop or start listening.

What it changes. What it doesn’t.

Your setup, carried forward.

Second Brain does not repair broken page code such as missing alt text or labels.

It does not use AI; AI lives only in the separate AccessAI Checker.

Browser zoom and operating system settings still matter, and browser-restricted pages may prevent extension changes.

Preferences apply within browser profiles where the extension is installed; browser sync behavior depends on your browser settings.
