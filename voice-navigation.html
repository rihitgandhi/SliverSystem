🗣️ Why Voice Navigation?

For millions of people, a mouse isn't a convenience — it's a wall. Motor impairments, RSI, temporary injuries, or simply hands full: the web was built assuming everyone clicks the same way. We built Voice Navigation because they don't.

✨ What It Does

Four commitments, not four checkboxes.

🧭

Smart Command Recognition

Say "scroll down," "click sign up," "go back" — natural language, not memorized syntax. The AI maps what you say to what you mean.

⚡

Real-time Response

No lag between speaking and acting. Commands execute as fast as you can say them.

🌐

Works on Any Site

No per-site setup, no compatibility whitelist. If it's a webpage, you can navigate it by voice.

🔒

Privacy by Design

Voice processing respects your data. Nothing about how you speak becomes something someone else owns.
