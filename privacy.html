Privacy & Data Use

What AccessAI processes—and what it does not.

AccessAI does not require an account. This page explains how the Second Brain extension and the Accessibility Checker handle information.

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Second Brain

Preferences stay within your browser environment.

  • Second Brain stores accessibility preferences through browser extension storage so it can apply supported settings on compatible pages.
  • Your browser may synchronize extension settings between browser profiles or devices, depending on your browser and sync settings.
  • AccessAI does not sell extension data or use it for advertising.
  • Voice Navigation uses speech-recognition capabilities supplied by your browser or operating system. AccessAI does not intentionally receive or store your voice audio, but your browser or operating-system provider may process speech under its own terms.

Accessibility Checker

A submitted public URL is processed to create a report.

  • The URL you submit is sent to the AccessAI server so the Checker can retrieve and render the publicly available page.
  • The Checker evaluates the downloaded markup and rendered page at three viewport widths. It does not log in to private pages or access content behind authentication.
  • Automated rules produce the findings. AI does not decide which violations exist or change their severity.
  • When AI explanations are available, AccessAI sends Azure OpenAI the page URL and a limited summary of verified scan data, such as rule names, counts, severity, sample selectors, existing remediation guidance, and manual-review checks. The full raw page HTML is not intentionally included in that AI prompt.
  • Do not submit URLs that contain private tokens, personal information, or content you are not authorized to analyze.

Storage & logs

AccessAI does not provide saved scan history.

The application does not create a user account or intentionally write completed Checker reports to an application database. Hosting, monitoring, browser, and AI providers may process standard technical information—such as IP address, request time, browser details, errors, and service logs—under their own security and retention practices.

Service providers

Some features rely on third-party infrastructure.

AccessAI may use website hosting and monitoring providers, Microsoft Azure OpenAI for optional Checker summaries, the Chrome Web Store for extension distribution, Google Fonts for typography, and unpkg for a page-scrolling library. These providers may receive ordinary technical request information when their services are used.

Questions

Ask before sharing anything sensitive.

For privacy questions, use the developer contact shown on the Second Brain Chrome Web Store listing.
