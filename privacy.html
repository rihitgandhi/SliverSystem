Privacy

Clear information about what leaves your browser.

AccessAI has no user accounts. This notice explains the limited information used by the public website, the Checker, and the Second Brain extension listing.

Accessibility appearance settings

The “Make this site work for you” controls save your selected text size, contrast, spacing, font, link, and motion preferences in this browser’s local storage. Those settings stay on your device and are used to update AccessAI pages.

Website Checker

When you submit a public website URL, AccessAI sends that URL to the Checker service so it can load the page and produce an accessibility report. Do not submit private, authenticated, or sensitive URLs. Reports describe the submitted public page and are not proof of full WCAG conformance.

Second Brain extension

The current Chrome Web Store disclosure states that the developer does not collect or use user data. Second Brain is designed to apply the preferences you choose while you browse; the public AccessAI website does not receive those extension preferences.

Third-party services

AccessAI uses Google Fonts for typography. The Second Brain demo connects to YouTube only after you choose to play it. Links to the Chrome Web Store and research sources take you to those services, where their own privacy terms apply.

Questions and updates

This notice may change when the products change. For extension support, use the developer support option on the Second Brain Chrome Web Store listing (opens in a new tab).
